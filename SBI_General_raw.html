What Is Health Insurance ?

Health Insurance, also called as medical insurance caters to expenses related to illnesses or injuries by either reimbursing your bills or directly paying the medical care provider. A comprehensive health insurance covers the cost of hospitalisation, day-care procedures, medical care at home (domiciliary hospitalisation), ambulance charges, maternity cover, OPD benefit, and more.

Having a health insurance plan ensures coverage against a range of diseases and offers the added advantage of enhancing tax savings. You can claim tax benefits for your health insurance premium under Section 80D of the Income Tax Act, 1961.

What’s Covered Under The Health Insurance Policy?

Hospitalisation  Expenses

All the basic expenses pertaining to hospitalisation – from room rent to medical procedures, surgery expenses, ICU costs, and doctor consultation fees are covered under Medical Insurance.

Pre- and Post-Hospitalisation Costs

Costs associated with diagnostic tests, biopsies, histopathology reports, physiotherapy, and doctor consultations, occurring before and after hospitalisation are covered with Health Insurance.

Day-care Treatments

Day care treatments that can be completed in less than 24 hours – which do not need you to be hospitalised overnight – are covered under the scope of Health Insurance.

AYUSH Treatments

Treatments besides Allopathic treatments, i.e., Ayurveda, Yoga, Unani, Siddha, and Homeopathy (AYUSH) treatments conducted by recognised and accredited healthcare facilities are covered with Health Insurance.

Domiciliary

With domiciliary hospitalization, you enjoy the benefit of receiving treatment and medical care from the comfort of your home in special circumstances.

Ambulance Charges

During the times of emergencies, you may need ambulance services to get to the hospital. The Health Insurance covers ambulance charges in such a situation.

Lifelong Renewability

Most Medical Insurance plans include a lifelong renewability cover, allowing you to get uninterrupted Health Insurance coverage, regardless of your age. You only need to renew your policy on time.

Organ Donor Expenses

In case that you need to undergo a surgery requiring organ transplant necessitated by organ failure, the medical and surgical costs associated with organ harvesting can be covered under the policy.

What’s Not Covered Under The Health Insurance Policy?

Self-inflicted Injuries

Coverage for the treatment of self-inflicted injuries or those sustained as a result of attempted suicide are not covered under the scope of Medical Insurance plans.

Cosmetic Surgeries

You cannot file an insurance claim if you choose to undergo any cosmetic treatment or plastic surgery, unless it is deemed necessary following accidents, burns, or cancer treatments, under Health Insurance.

Substance Abuse

The costs associated with treatments resulting from the consumption or abuse of intoxicating substances, including drugs and alcohol, are not covered by Medical Insurance.

Experimental/Overseas Treatments

Health Insurance is not liable to cover the costs associated with experimental treatments. You may also not file a claim if you choose to seek medical treatments overseas, unless specified under policy terms.

Injuries Sustained from  Adventure Sports

If you sustain physical injuries as a result of participating in adventure sports activities like parasailing, paragliding, skiing, sky diving, etc., you cannot file an insurance claim with Medical Insurance for the treatment costs.

Sterility, infertility, & Gender Change Procedures

Expenses arising from optional medical procedures such as sterility treatment, infertility treatment, in-vitro fertilisation (IVF) as well as gender change procedures are excluded from the scope of coverage under Health Insurance.

War-related Injuries

Injuries and treatment costs associated with wars and war-like events as well as other acts of communal disharmony, which affect all masses, are not covered under Medical Insurance.

Breach of Law

Treatments arising due to you breaching policy terms, like consuming drugs, driving while intoxicated and sustaining injuries, etc., are outside the scope of Health Insurance coverage.

How To Choose A Health Insurance Plan?

Consider the Age

Based on your age, you may need different coverage amount. Your age can be an important factor affecting your health since as you age, you tend to develop certain medical conditions associated with ageing. You can choose a Health Insurance plan that caters to your needs best according to your age.

Consider the Sum Insured

With medical inflation being a reality, it is critical to choose a health policy with a considerably higher sum insured to remain financially secured well into the future. This way, when you need to file a claim many years later, you do not have to worry about the rising medical costs and the financial burden it may bring.

Assess the Coverage Terms

Beyond the basic pre- and post-hospitalisation costs, some Health Insurance plans offer coverage for maternity, ambulance charges cover, home health care cover, and OPD coverage, among other things. You should opt for a plan that caters to your requirements the best.

Consider the Reputation

Factors like our customer care, the claim settlement ratio, solvency ratio, & the speed at which services are provided play a role in your overall experience. So, you must assess the same before buying your health policy. SBI General boasts a high claim settlement ratio along with speedy services such as customer care.

Check the Add-on Riders

If a person wishes to customise a policy to include certain possible medical conditions and the expenses associated with them, they can easily do so with the range of add-on riders offered. These riders provide added benefits and coverage for conditions specified in the policy.

Assess the Waiting Periods

Every Health Insurance policy comes with a fixed waiting period that may last anywhere from 30 days to 4 years. During this time, you cannot raise a claim despite having a Health Plan. Most of our Health Insurance plans offer a waiting period of only 30 days.

Why Buy A Health Insurance Plan At An Early Age?

Lower Premiums

Purchasing health insurance at an early age often results in relatively lower premiums. Insurers consider youth and good health, providing cost advantages that can be maintained throughout the policy term.

Financial Security

Early adoption of health insurance ensures financial security against any unforeseen medical expenses. Health Insurance acts as a safety net, preventing the burden of healthcare costs from affecting your financial well-being from a young age.

Pre-existing Condition Coverage

Early insurance safeguards against denial or higher premiums for pre-existing conditions, ensuring coverage for potential medical needs. Obtaining insurance early offers reassurance and financial stability as you age, minimizing the risk of coverage issues or increased premiums.

Long-term Benefits

Investing in health insurance at an early age yields long-term benefits. You can potentially enjoy the continuity, additional benefits, and security as you progress through different life stages.

How To Save Tax With Your SBI General Health Insurance Policy?

Under Section 80D

Under Section 80D of the Income Tax Act of India, 1961, you can claim deductions up to:

Beneficiaries of SBI General InsuranceAnnual Deduction Amount
Yourself, your spouse, and your children₹25,000
Yourself, your spouse, your children, and your parents under the age of 60 years₹50,000
Yourself, your spouse, your children, and your senior citizen parents₹75,000
Yourself (over the age of 60 years), your spouse, your children, and your parents₹100,000

Under Section 80DDB

Under this section of the IT Act, you can claim tax deductions:

  • On Health Insurance and related-expenses incurred towards the treatment of specific critical illnesses, as stated under Section 80DDB.
  • The maximum annual tax deduction limit for such expenses is capped at ₹40,000 or the actual premium amount paid, whichever is lower.
  • In case of policies taken for the critical treatment of senior citizens, the maximum deduction you can claim is capped at ₹1 lakh.

Under 80DD

Section 80DD of the IT Act allows you to claim tax deductions for:

  • The medical costs incurred for yourself as a disabled individual, or any dependents persons suffering from physical or mental disabilities.
  • Under this section, you can claim a maximum deduction of up to ₹75,000 per annum.

Eligibility Criteria To Purchase A Health Insurance Policy In India

  • You should be a resident of India.
  • You should be in within the age group of 18 to 65 years, or as specified by SBI General Insurance, in accordance with the plan you choose.

Factors That Affect Your Health Insurance Premium Rates

Age

We usually charge a lower premium when people are younger and healthier, compared to when they buy insurance in their 40s.

Sum Insured

Your sum insured is a key factor for determining the premiums you pay. For a lower sum insured, you may have to pay lower premiums and vice versa. You should consider your sum insured as a factor because this amount covers your medical expenses.

Coverage Type

The higher the coverage you opt for, the higher the premium rates charged. Furthermore, coverage types such as individual and family floater plans also play a determining role in premium rates as the premiums for each plan varies.

Policy Duration

A long-term health plan can pay for all your medical expenses incurred during the policy period. The premium paid for this plan tends to even out over the longer term. That is to say, the cost for single hospitalization can be in lakhs; however, the premiums paid may be significantly lower.

Health Conditions

If a person has any pre-existing medical conditions, the premium rates may be higher than usual for the provided coverage. However, all the expenses incurred due to the pre-existing condition may be covered at this premium easily.

Lifestyle Choices

If an individual routinely smokes, drinks or indulges in other similar practices, their insurance premiums could be higher, if they are deemed eligible for coverage.

Geographical Location

If a person resides in a small town, where costs are lower, the premium rates may be lower too, compared to those charged from residents in metro cities.
