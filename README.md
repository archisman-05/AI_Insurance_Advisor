# 🧠 AI\_Insurance\_Advisor

AI\_Insurance\_Advisor is a smart and user-friendly virtual assistant that helps users find the **best medical insurance policies** tailored to their specific needs. With its advanced AI capabilities, the system can understand user concerns, compare multiple policies, and break down complex health insurance jargon into simple, easy-to-understand language.

---

## 🚀 Features

* 🤖 **AI-Powered Query Understanding**: Users can describe their health insurance needs, problems, or concerns in natural language.
* 📋 **Policy Recommendations**: Based on real-time data, the advisor suggests the most suitable medical insurance policies available in the market.
* ⚖️ **Policy Comparison**: Users can compare two or more insurance policies side-by-side based on premium, coverage, waiting period, network hospitals, claim process, and more.
* 🧾 **Term Explanation**: Ask about any complex insurance-related term (like co-pay, deductible, waiting period, etc.) and get a clear, concise explanation.
* ⏱️ **Real-Time Market Updates** *(if integrated with live APIs)*: Ensures that policy suggestions are always based on the latest available offerings.


## 🧑‍💻 How to Use

1. **Start the AI Advisor**.
2. **Input your issue** – e.g., *"I’m looking for a family plan that covers maternity and critical illness."*
3. The AI will analyze your need and suggest best-fit policies.
4. Use **"Compare Policies"** to input names or URLs of two or more policies.
5. Ask questions like *“What is the difference between co-pay and deductible?”* for instant explanations.
